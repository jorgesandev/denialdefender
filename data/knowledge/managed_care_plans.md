# Pillar 1: Managed Care Plan Structures

## HMO (Health Maintenance Organization)
Network restrictions, gatekeeper referral requirements, in-network-only coverage rules, and the appeal grounds these rules typically support.

## PPO (Preferred Provider Organization)
Tiered network reimbursement, out-of-network exception pathways, and balance-billing protections relevant to appeals on out-of-network denials.

## EPO and POS (Exclusive Provider Organization & Point of Service)
Hybrid plan structures and the specific appeal arguments that succeed against each.

## Medicare Advantage
Two-level appeal pathway (plan reconsideration, then independent QIO review), CMS-mandated timelines, and the higher overturn rates that make MA appeals especially worth filing.

## Medicaid Managed Care
State-by-state variation in appeal procedures and external review rights.
