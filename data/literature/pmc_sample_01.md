# PubMed Central OA Excerpt

**PMID:** 12345678
**Title:** Efficacy of Intervention vs Conservative Management for Chronic Conditions
**Journal:** Journal of Medical Necessity
**Year:** 2023

## Abstract
**Background:** Chronic conditions often fail to respond to conservative management, requiring surgical or advanced pharmacological interventions.
**Methods:** A randomized controlled trial comparing conservative therapy (physical therapy and NSAIDs) with advanced intervention in 500 patients.
**Results:** Patients receiving the advanced intervention showed a 75% improvement in symptoms and functional capacity at 6 months compared to 20% in the conservative group (p<0.001).
**Conclusion:** For patients who have failed at least 6 weeks of conservative management, advanced intervention is the established standard of care and provides significant clinical benefit.
